# Decisions

The choices the owner has made (the log) and those not yet taken (open). Requirements cite them as "D13" or "open decision O7".
A decision is never rewritten: a later one supersedes it and the old one is marked "superseded by Dn". When an open decision is
taken, it moves to the log as the next D-number, the requirement it affects is edited in `10-requirements.md`, and its entry leaves
the open list.

| D117 | The `override` of the include (owner decision of 2026-11-13, brought forward from D116; the note is `docs/_notes/include-design.md`). - **O76** Router: choosing the model by the complexity of the request (raised by the owner, 2026-11-12, who asked for an analysis and then for a plugin in `router`; no work is planned until the points below are decided). For the future implementation, owner request of 2026-11-12: the strategy receives the real request and not only the length.
